# TriHerm

**Software and AI systems for real business operations.**

TriHerm builds practical systems that help businesses improve workflows, automation, customer acquisition, and operational execution.

Our immediate focus is simple: **build useful systems, deliver measurable value, and learn from real operating constraints.**

## TriHerm Labs

[TriHerm Labs](https://github.com/TriHerm) is our open-source and experimental engineering initiative. It is where we publish products, developer tools, and technical experiments that are useful on their own while informing broader systems work.

### Current projects

- [**TypeOpen**](https://github.com/TriHerm/TypeOpen) — a lightweight, open-source Markdown editor for macOS with live editing, source mode, navigation, and document export.
- [**Cutmark**](https://github.com/TriHerm/cutmark) — a local-first video production workspace for people and AI agents, with native macOS workflows, MCP access, and support for multiple AI runners.
- [**Homebrew Tap**](https://github.com/TriHerm/homebrew-tap) — distribution for TriHerm Labs macOS releases.

## Direction

As AI agents become more capable of acting on behalf of people and organizations, businesses will need better infrastructure for discovery, authorization, transactions, execution, and verification.

TriHerm is exploring that longer-term coordination layer from the bottom up — starting with real software, real workflows, and existing business systems rather than assuming they should be replaced.

## How we build

- Revenue and customer value before speculative infrastructure.
- Real workflows before abstract protocol design.
- Reliability, auditability, and human authority as system constraints.
- Open standards and replaceable components where practical.
- Public work should be useful, inspectable, and maintainable.
