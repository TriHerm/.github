# Code of Conduct

TriHerm open-source projects are intended to support constructive technical collaboration.

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, discuss ideas and implementations on their merits, provide actionable feedback, respect different levels of experience, and avoid harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or deliberate disruption.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that undermine a safe and productive project environment.

When raising a conduct concern, provide enough context for maintainers to evaluate it without unnecessarily exposing private information. Project-specific policies take precedence over this organization-wide default.
